Family Calendar

Configure a shared calendar to show simplified "blocks" of your activities to family members. They'll see when you're busy without seeing the details of your tasks.

To find your calendar ID:

  1. Go to Google Calendar
  2. Click on the three dots next to your shared calendar
  3. Select "Settings and sharing"
  4. Scroll down to "Integrate calendar"
  5. Copy the "Calendar ID" (ends with @group.calendar.google.com)
